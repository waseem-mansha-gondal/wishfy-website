We set up the technical SEO foundations that traditional search engines
*and* AI-powered search experiences (Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT search,
Perplexity) need to find, parse, and cite your content correctly.

## What we cover

- Structured data: `Organization`, `WebSite`, `BreadcrumbList`, page-type
  specific schema
- Canonical URLs, sitemap, `robots.txt`, `llms.txt`
- Meta + OG + Twitter cards
- Content review for E-E-A-T signals
