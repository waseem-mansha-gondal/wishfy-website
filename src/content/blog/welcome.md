## What you'll find here

We write about three things:

1. **Shipping** — practical posts on getting marketing sites from zero to
   live without a six-week detour.
2. **Performance** — what we measure, what we ignore, and why.
3. **The boring middle** — content workflows, CMS choices, and the
   organisational stuff that decides whether a site stays good.

## How this post was written

It's plain Markdown in `src/content/blog/welcome.md`. The frontmatter at
the top of the file controls the title, date, and tags on this page. Add
a new file in the same folder to publish a new post — no engineering
required.
